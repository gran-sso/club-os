## Inputs handled later through configuration/qualification

- Actual fees, plan benefits, room/court schedules, cancellation rules and tax mappings.
- Verified member import, opening prepaid balances, stock quantities/values and financial opening balances.
- Tally product/release, company and chart mappings, connector host and test company access.
- Card/reader/controller/biometric model, supported API or SDK, connectivity, enrolment and revocation behaviour.
- Digital payment provider or verified manual terminal/UPI process, settlement references and fees.
- Contractor bank details, settlement allocation/tax rules and any actual deductions agreed in the contract.
- Print equipment, silent-printing requirement, backup/recovery settings and real rollout details.

These are not blocking discovery questions for the development phase. Keep modules configurable and mark unqualified external integrations accurately.
